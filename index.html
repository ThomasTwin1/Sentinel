USER ACCEPTANCE TESTING

Recommended Test Scenarios

  1. Monthly due date: Use July 1 and confirm the due date is August 1.
  2. Month-end handling: Use January 31 and confirm February resolves to the last valid day of the month.
  3. Daily weekend rule: Use a Friday conducted date and confirm the next due date is Monday.
  4. Daily holiday rule: Add Monday as a training holiday, use the preceding Friday, and confirm the due date moves to Tuesday.
  5. Signed Days to Due: Confirm future requirements show positive numbers, today shows 0, and overdue requirements show negative numbers.
  6. Due-soon status: Confirm Weekly uses 1–2 days, Monthly uses 1–7 days, and Quarterly uses 1–14 days.
  7. CSV import: Import a sanitized FPAR export and confirm the latest dated record is used for each facility, regardless of workflow status.
  8. Sorting: Confirm Urgency places the most overdue first and Building Number places the lowest building first.
  9. Duplicate warning: Add two facilities with the same name on the same installation and confirm a warning appears.
  10. Inactive facility: Mark a facility inactive and confirm it disappears from the dashboard.
  11. Backup and restore: Export JSON, delete or change a record, then restore the file.
Document defects in GitHub: Record the test setup, expected result, actual result, screenshots, and the decision made after testing.
