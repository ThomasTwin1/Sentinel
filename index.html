USER ACCEPTANCE TESTING

Recommended Test Scenarios

  1. Monthly due date: Use July 1 and confirm the due date is August 1.
  2. Month-end handling: Use January 31 and confirm February resolves to the last valid day of the month.
  3. Daily weekend rule: Use a Friday conducted date and confirm the next due date is Monday.
  4. Daily holiday rule: Add Monday as a training holiday, use the preceding Friday, and confirm the due date moves to Tuesday.
  5. Signed Days to Due: Confirm future requirements show positive numbers, today shows 0, and overdue requirements show negative numbers.
  6. Due-soon status: Confirm Weekly uses 1–2 days, Monthly uses 1–7 days, and Quarterly uses 1–14 days.
  7. CSV import: Import a sanitized FPAR export and confirm the latest dated record is used for each facility, regardless of workflow status.
  8. Sorting: Confirm Urgency places the most overdue first and Building Number places the lowest building first.
  9. Duplicate warning: Add two facilities with the same name on the same installation and confirm a warning appears.
  10. Inactive facility: Mark a facility inactive and confirm it disappears from the dashboard.
  11. MILSANS CSV: Import fictional inspection-result history and confirm the cards use the latest completed inspection for each facility.
  12. MILSANS interaction: Tap the due-status cards or Inspection Rating Key and confirm the MILSANS Dashboard filters and scrolls to the first matching facility.
  13. MILSANS inspector: Filter by inspector and confirm the inspector shown comes from the DOEHRS Surveyor field. When that field is blank, confirm Sentinel says it was not listed instead of assuming the DOEHRS record creator performed the inspection.
  14. MILSANS interval status: Confirm a monthly record becomes Missed after one calendar month and a quarterly record becomes Missed after three calendar months unless a required follow-up date applies.
  15. MILSANS month filter: Select a scheduled month and confirm only facilities assigned to that month remain visible.
  16. Missed inspection history: Use a quarterly facility last completed in February and an as-of date after August 25. Confirm both May 25 and August 25 appear until a newer completed inspection is imported.
  17. Assigned team: Confirm the locally assigned team or lead appears next to the last inspector on each MILSANS card.
  18. Printing: Open each dashboard separately. Tap Print FPAR Cards or Print MILSANS Cards and confirm the iPhone print sheet opens with clean facility cards.
  19. Scheduled Date: Confirm a required follow-up date takes priority; otherwise Scheduled Date is the 25th of the scheduled month.
  20. Missed Month: Confirm missed facilities display the exact 25th of the month in which the required monthly or quarterly inspection was due.
  21. TSFC letter grades: Confirm Fully=A, Substantially=B, Partially=C, and Noncompliant=F. Confirm no D grade appears.
  22. Backup and restore: Export an encrypted .sentinel backup, delete or change a record, then verify and restore the file with its passphrase.
Document defects in GitHub: Record the test setup, expected result, actual result, screenshots, and the decision made after testing.
